Write a 20-45 second YouTube Short script.

Style:
- Fast, punchy, detached, and unsettling
- Immediate recognition
- No intro, no branding, no buildup

Structure:
- 0-2s: Immediate recognition hook (e.g., "You've [behavior] without realizing why.")
